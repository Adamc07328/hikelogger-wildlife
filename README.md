# hikelogger-wildlife

Species photos for the Hike Logger app, with the manifest the app reads.

- `manifest.json` lists every image: its species (`taxonId`, `scientificName`), file, size, SHA-256,
  author, licence and Commons source. Image URLs are `baseUrl` + `file`.
- `images/` holds one WebP per species, at most 800 px wide, named after the taxonId
  (`col:32ZQ7` is `images/col_32ZQ7.webp`).
- `CREDITS.md` credits each photo's author and licence.

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons under CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. They are
picked by hand and generated by the HikeLogger repo's `tools/commons_candidates.py` and
`tools/commons_apply.py` (see its `docs/wildlife-images.md`). Don't edit the files by hand.

Images were resized and converted to WebP.
